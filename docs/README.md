# Documentation

- [history.md](history.md): how the chip came to speak, day by day, and who found what; and every
  device known to have used the SSI-263, year by year.
- [die.md](die.md): reading the phoneme ROM off the die photographs, and what it means.
- [measurements.md](measurements.md): measuring a real SSI-263 in a Braille Lite 2000, and
  the lessons that cost the most.
- [engine.md](engine.md): the chip model, part by part, and what it doesn't do yet.
- [front-ends.md](front-ends.md): each device's own firmware and the machines it runs on.
- [open-questions.md](open-questions.md): what isn't known yet, and where you can help.
- [sources.md](sources.md): data sheets, papers, patents, software and people.
- [mockingboard-firmware.md](mockingboard-firmware.md): the Mockingboard voices' firmware: which disks, what
  the importers recognize, and which archives keep them.

The working letters these pages were drawn from stay with the project's private files.
`src/HOLDOUT.md` is the engine's own change log, with every look at the hold-out.
